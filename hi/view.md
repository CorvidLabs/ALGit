# View

## Intent

After a push, the same person starts a page that stays on their computer and reads the repository the way they would read it on a forge: the commits, the files, and the diff of a commit.


## Criteria

- **VIEW-1**  I can start a page on my own machine and read the commits, files, and diffs of what I pushed.
- **VIEW-2**  The page I open shows the files and the commits, and it looks like the CorvidLabs design system.
